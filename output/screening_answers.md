## ScaleMyMealPrep — E-Commerce Account Manager

**Q: A short answer describing a Shopify issue you personally diagnosed and fixed, and one Meta Ads campaign change you made and why.**

*Shopify:* On one of my DTC brands, I noticed post-purchase revenue per order was low. I diagnosed that the checkout experience ended at the confirmation page with no further engagement. I implemented a post-purchase upsell flow and built complementary product bundles directly in Shopify, which increased average order value and improved backend profitability without increasing ad spend.

*Meta Ads:* A campaign I was running had plateaued in ROAS. I restructured it by separating audience avatars into distinct ad sets and systematically testing different desires and creative angles per avatar. Once I identified the highest-performing combinations, I cut the underperformers and reallocated budget to the winners — which broke the plateau and improved ROAS.

**Q: Confirmation of your hands-on (not managerial/oversight) experience with Shopify and Meta Ads.**
Yes — I have personally managed both platforms hands-on. I build and update Shopify storefronts directly (product pages, collections, promotions, upsells, bundles) and I personally set up, monitor, and optimize Meta Ads campaigns including creative testing and audience targeting.

**Q: How many clients have you personally handled in your previous roles?**
